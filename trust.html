App Verification Guide

How to Trust the App

  1. When you first open the app you see a notification that the app isn't trusted on your device. You can dismiss this message.
  2. Tap Settings > General > VPN & Device Management > Aqua-Aerobic Systems, Inc.
  3. Tap Trust "Aqua-Aerobic Systems, Inc"
  4. Tap Trust prompt to verify that you trust the app.
    (If prompted: "Allow Apps from Aqua-Aerobic Systems, Inc."?, tap "Allow & Restart")
    5. Apple now requires your iPhone to reverify periodically to maintain trust.
  5. Upon restart you will be prompted to swipe Up and install new profile.
  6. Open app and Tap Allow Notifications
  7. You are now ready to use the app. Enjoy!
